# AgroScope Kurdistan

A free Windows program for farmers, agronomists and students in the Kurdistan Region. Draw your field on the map or
load your area, pick a date and tick what you want to know: AgroScope Kurdistan downloads the newest open satellite
and weather data for that area (FAO WaPOR, Copernicus Sentinel-2, Open-Meteo/ECMWF, ERA5, GloFAS, Copernicus DEM,
ESA WorldCover) and makes maps, charts and a report as JPEG files, in Kurdish or English: crop health, water stress
and missing water, water use, irrigated land, crop calendar, rain and drought (SPI), land and crop suitability,
16-day weather, irrigation forecast, growth stages, seasonal outlook, climate change and river flow, plus maps of
the whole Kurdistan Region. No installation and no account.

**[Download AgroScope Kurdistan for Windows](https://github.com/kchomani/AgroScope-Kurdistan/releases/latest/download/AgroScope-Kurdistan-Windows-x64.zip)**
(the newest version), or see [all versions](https://github.com/kchomani/AgroScope-Kurdistan/releases).

Right-click the zip, choose Extract All and wait until unzipping has finished, then double-click `AgroScope.exe` in
the AgroScope folder. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**; the
program is free and not code-signed. It needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and an internet connection.

Download page: **https://agroscope-kurdistan.netlify.app/**

This repository holds only the download page and the compiled program releases; the program's source code is not
published.

Created by **Kaifi Chomani** (کەیفی چۆمانی), University of Raparin, founder of
[Egeocate Lab for GIS & Remote Sensing](https://egeocate.netlify.app/) — [CV](https://claude.ai/artifact/1pkEazJnxJPDjCm2udXrPu).

Free for education, research and farmers, provided as is without warranty. The results are satellite and model
estimates: check in the field before big decisions. The data credits and licences are listed in the program's Data
sources tab and printed on every map.
